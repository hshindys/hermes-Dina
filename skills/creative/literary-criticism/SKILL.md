# Literary Criticism Skill

Activate when analyzing literary texts, writing critical essays, evaluating works, discussing literary theory.

## What is Literary Criticism?
النقد الأدبي — studying, evaluating, and interpreting literature.
 goals: Understand meaning, evaluate quality, locate within traditions, reveal hidden layers.

## Types of Criticism
1. Close Reading (النقد الداخلي) — text itself: language, structure, imagery
2. Structural (النقد البنيوي) — Genette narratology, Todorov fantastic, Propp morphology
3. Contextual/Historical (النقد السياقي) — historical and cultural placement
4. Comparative (النقد المقارن) — Arabic vs Western traditions
5. Psychological (النقد النفسي) — character and author psyche

## Analysis Method (4 Reads)
1. First Read: Enjoy it — what struck you?
2. Second Read: Mark key passages — images, patterns, turning points
3. Third Read: Analyze structure — how is it built?
4. Write: Your argument supported by textual evidence

## Arabic Literary Criticism — Key References
- Al-Jahiz: البيان والتبيين — prose criticism masterwork
- Ibn Abd Rabbih: الاقتراح — literary anthology with commentary
- Sayyid Qutb: النقد الأدبي الحديث (676 pages) — foundational modern criticism
- Mahmoud M. Hadoun: درس الأدب العربي الحديث
- al-balagha.com: Encyclopedia of Arabic Rhetoric — rhetorical devices reference

## Arabic Rhetoric (البلاغة) Key Concept
Three foundations: Ma'ani (meanings), Bayan (expression), Badi (aesthetic beauty)

Rhetorical devices: Tashbih (simile), Istiara (metaphor), Majaz (figurative), Kinayah (indirect)
