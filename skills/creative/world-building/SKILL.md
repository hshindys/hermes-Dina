# World-Building for Fiction Writing

Activate when building fictional settings or creating new story universes for novels and short stories.

## The 5-Step Method
1. Leave your world — recognize your fictional world is distinct from reality
2. Determine what you cant do in reality — set your rules
3. Create characters WITHIN the world — how do they interact with it?
4. All Things Go — everything must be consistent within your universe
5. Conquer your world — you own it, research it, dominate it

## World-Building Checklist
- Geography, climate, architecture
- Political/economic/social systems
- Religion, customs, food, clothing, language
- Sensory detail (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste)
- How characters interact with physical + social world

## Jinn World-Building (Arabic Fantasy Specialization)
- Jinn = parallel civilization invisible to humans
- Kingdoms by element (air, water, earth, fire)
- Rules governed by Quran/Hadith — cannot contradict divine law
- Conflict engine: Jinn interacting with humans
- Language: Archaic/formal for Jinn, colloquial for humans
